# CS-255-12578-M01-System-Analysis-and-Design-2026-
DriverPass Project Summary

The DriverPass project was created for a client who wanted a system to help students prepare for their driving tests. The system needed to allow students to schedule driving lessons, access online practice tests and study materials, manage their accounts, and track their progress. It also needed features for instructors and administrative staff to manage schedules, student information, and lesson records.

What I Did Well

One thing I did particularly well was identifying the client's requirements and organizing them into clear business requirements and system design documents. I also created UML diagrams that helped show how different users would interact with the system. This project helped me better understand how to translate customer needs into a system design that could be used to develop software.

What I Would Improve

If I could revise one part of my work, I would improve the business requirements and system design documents by providing more detail and making stronger connections between requirements and system features. I would also review the UML diagrams more carefully to ensure they included all necessary information and accurately represented every process within the system.

How I Interpreted the User's Needs

I interpreted the user's needs by carefully reviewing the interview and identifying the features and functions the client wanted. I then incorporated those requirements into the business requirements document and system design. Considering user needs is important because the success of a system depends on whether it solves the user's problems and provides the functionality they expect. If user needs are overlooked, the system may not be effective or useful.

My Approach to Software Design

When designing software, I begin by gathering and analyzing requirements to understand the goals of the system. Next, I create models and diagrams that help visualize how the system will function and how users will interact with it. In the future, I will continue using techniques such as requirements analysis, UML diagrams, use cases, and system modeling to develop designs that meet both business and user needs. These strategies help ensure that the final system is organized, functional, and aligned with customer expectations.
